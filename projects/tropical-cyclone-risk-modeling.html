Tropical Cyclone Risk Modeling

Undergraduate Research Assistant · Srikrishnan Lab, Cornell University

Project overview

This research advances coastal damage and resilience analysis by connecting modeled tropical cyclone hazards with the Coastal Impact and Adaptation Model (CIAM).

Work In Progress

  • Porting a tropical cyclone risk prediction model from Python to Julia.
  • Generating storm genesis, track, and intensification-rate parameters from ERA5 data.

Future Work

  • Integrating modeled hazard outputs directly into CIAM.
